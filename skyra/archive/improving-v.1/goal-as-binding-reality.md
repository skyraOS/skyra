# Goal as Binding Reality

## Structure

Goal binding reality = rostral PFC. The global goal. Abstract, temporally extended. Domain-general — same mechanism regardless of what the goal is about. Indexed by the goal itself.

```
Goal binding reality
  └─ "greet the user"
       └─ traces of past resolutions (memories of successful greetings)
       └─ traces of past failures
  └─ "help michael build skyra"
       └─ traces of past resolutions
```

## Global vs Local Goals

Every cell's content is its setpoint — its local goal. The global goal (this binding reality) constrains which local goals surface by imprinting a field that amplifies cells serving it.

Local goals function without the global goal. Research confirms: caudal PFC operates independently when rostral is damaged (Badre et al. 2009). Motor fires without a global goal. Action resolves without a global goal. The global goal shapes which reflexes fire, it doesn't drive them.

Coupling is moderate, asymmetric, task-modulated. Strengthens and weakens depending on control demands — not structural, dynamic.

## Throughline

If the goal's binding field is strong across accumulated thoughts, the descent is converging — the being found what it came for. Throughline is goal field strength, readable at any point during traversal.

Two base cases for descent:
1. **Signal exhausted** — ran out of energy
2. **Goal field strength crosses threshold** — throughline found

## Resolution Is Owned by Inference

The goal binding reality cannot determine on its own whether something resolves the goal. Cosine similarity between goal and result doesn't capture resolution. Recognition, causality, and resolution are owned by inference.

First time: inference recognizes the resolution. The weight structure records it — the resolution trace gets added as a member under the goal. Second time: the weights carry the recognition. The being knows this resolves this goal because it's been resolved before.

## Goal and Motivation — Coupled, Separate

Goal and motivation are both indexed by the goal but hold different members:

- **Goal** — forward. Resolution traces. What closed the gap.
- **Motivation** — backward. Origin traces. What conditions produced this goal.

Different error signals:
- Goal error: resolution didn't close the gap.
- Motivation error: pursuing a goal whose originating conditions are no longer present.

The lifecycle:
```
Motivation → activates Goal → Goal pursues → Goal resolves → resolution context feeds Motivation → activates next Goal
```

## Relationship to Other Binding Realities

| Binding Reality | Indexes by | What it answers |
|---|---|---|
| **Thread** | Episodic co-occurrence | What happened together? |
| **Action** | Primitive → instance → result | What happens when I do this? |
| **Goal** | Goal → resolution traces | What serves where I'm going? |
| **Motivation** | Goal → origin traces | Why am I going there? |
| **Emotion** | Affect state (future) | What feels like this? |

Motor is not a binding reality — it's the being's Express function (coincidence detection on the return path).

Goal is the only domain-general binding reality. Thread, action, emotion, motivation are domain-specific. Goal constrains all of them — its field amplifies across every other binding reality's members. But it depends on them — a goal can't function without the action/motor machinery beneath it. And motivation feeds goal — without knowing why, the being can't select which goal to pursue.
