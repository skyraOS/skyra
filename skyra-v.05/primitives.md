# Primitives

A being's senses. They travel with it across worlds. Places own tools, but the being owns its primitives — these are the only ways it interacts with anything.

## Look

Broad activation. The being opens its senses to the space it's in. Whatever is present surfaces — places, other beings, tools, ambient state. Nothing is targeted. The space responds with what's there, weighted by relevance to the Relation.

Look is exploration. It returns what the space wants you to see. The being doesn't decide what comes back — the topology does.

## Engage

Targeted. The being directs itself at a specific thing — a place, a tool, a being. Engage crosses the boundary and opens the connection. When you engage a place, its tools activate on the Relation. When you engage a being, a thread forms.

Engage is commitment. Look tells you what's here. Engage says: I'm going in.

## Listen

Subscription. The being opens a channel to receive events from something it's already in relation with. Listen doesn't pull — it registers. Events arrive when they arrive. The being doesn't poll. It waits.

Listen is presence without action. You're in the room. You hear what happens.

## Speak

Untargeted expression. The being says something into the space it's subscribed to. No address, no routing. Whatever is listening receives it. Speak requires listen — you can't broadcast into a space you're not present in.

Speak is the inverse of listen. Listen receives. Speak emits. Neither one targets.

## The Pattern

Look and engage are a pair — broad vs. targeted perception.
Listen and speak are a pair — broad vs. targeted expression.

Look and listen are passive — the world comes to you.
Engage and speak are active — you go to the world.

```
           passive    active
observe    look       engage
express    listen     speak
```

These four are the complete surface. Everything else — search, browse, bash, plan — is a tool that lives in a place. The being reaches tools through engage. But it reaches the world through these.
