# Symbols That No Longer Stand in dnav3

## Method

A criticism no longer stands when dnav3 materially changes the architecture in
the precise place the criticism attacked. This does not mean the surrounding
topic is finished. It means repeating the original criticism would ignore
evidence and become a fault of the critic.

Five original symbols no longer stand in their original form.

## ⟦FEEDBACK↺⟧ Feedback is not yet sufficiently independent

This criticism no longer stands against dnav3's exact consequence-learning
path.

In dnav2, an LLM proposed causal structure and other LLM calls judged whether
the proposed causes, predictions, and actions agreed with later evidence. The
same kind of semantic engine could author the hypothesis, interpret the
observation, and grade the relationship. The learning signal bottomed out in a
model opinion.

dnav3 changes the representation so that exact grading no longer requires such
an opinion.

An expression contains an act and a falsifiable expectation. The expectation
names a subject, predicate, expected object, confidence, responsible act, and
deadline. After the responsible act completes, a later eligible world-derived
observation at the same address resolves the expectation. The comparison is
arithmetic. The model is not called during exact grading and cannot choose the
grade.

Several protections make the independence substantive:

- self-authored marks have zero fidelity and cannot grade world claims;
- predictions cannot settle before their responsible acts complete;
- an earlier observation cannot be consumed retroactively after arming;
- old-episode predictions cannot settle in a replacement world;
- unobserved expectations remain unknown rather than becoming false;
- matured bets trigger targeted perception so evidence is actively obtained;
- interpretation correctness and action consequence use separate addresses;
- the world never receives the being's prediction and only reports its own
  state.

The raw-observation encoding boundary still deserves scrutiny, especially when
unstructured inputs must become triples. That is a different and narrower
criticism. It does not justify continuing to say that dnav3's exact feedback is
self-judged.

Status: **answered for exact claims; a decomposed observation-encoding question
remains elsewhere**.

## ⟦LAW≈CONVENTION⟧ Architectural laws are still partly conventions

This criticism no longer accurately characterizes dnav3's core epistemic laws.

In v.05, major laws lived in prompts and behavioral expectations. A sufficiently
confused model could ignore the speaking protocol, misuse context, or violate an
intended boundary. Some declared physics was inactive or disabled.

dnav3 moves its important laws into types, indexes, temporal checks, provenance,
and call topology:

- Expression cannot directly write observed world state.
- Only the observation path writes raw world facts.
- Marks cannot grade because their fidelity is zero.
- Exact grades are computed without an inference call.
- A prediction must name responsible acts and a deadline or validation rejects
  it.
- Joint predictions arm only after every responsible act completes.
- Episode identity prevents one world from adjudicating another world's claims.
- Observation sequence and arming time prevent retroactive evidence use.
- Affordances, rather than the model, determine whether a claim is exactly
  measurable.
- Interpretation and consequence claims have distinct falsification addresses.
- Held-out calibration rejects self-labeling and selection/evaluation overlap.

These laws continue to hold when the model is confused, absent, or adversarial.
The model may emit unusable content, but it cannot use that content to bypass
the settlement rules.

Not every aspiration is substrate-enforced. Persistent intention, complete
blind observation encoding, and cross-package runtime integration remain open.
But the original blanket criticism—that the architecture's important laws are
still prompt conventions—fails to acknowledge the main achievement of dnav3.

Status: **answered for the core epistemic laws**.

## ⟦CLAIM¬FALSIFIED⟧ The abstractions remain difficult to falsify at the product level

This criticism no longer stands against dnav3's central research claim.

dnav3 states a result that can fail:

```text
passes-to-closure  ↓
prediction error   ↓
```

It also explains why one curve is insufficient. Fewer passes without lower
error indicates premature confidence. Lower error without fewer passes
indicates search without learning. That is an unusually clear rejection
criterion for an architecture at this stage.

The code defines relevant measurements and exposes failure states:

- attempted cycles and successful closures are separate counters;
- unresolved runs are excluded from passes-to-closure rather than disguised as
  successes;
- confidence is scored against a measured base rate;
- unknown and void outcomes do not silently become false;
- interpretation correctness is measured separately from consequence success;
- malformed or inadmissible predictions are retained as rejections;
- exact claims can be wrong and receive negative score;
- calibration may remain insufficient, inert, or anti-diagnostic rather than
  being promoted.

The project has not yet passed its own decisive test under a live longitudinal
evaluation. That belongs under `⟦Δ≠DEV⟧`. A claim can be falsifiable before it is
validated. Continuing to call it unfalsifiable would confuse lack of evidence
with lack of a defeating condition.

Broader product ideas such as selfhood, relationship, or a living world still
require their own falsification criteria. The dnav3 cognitive thesis itself now
has one.

Status: **answered for the central dnav3 learning thesis**.

## ⟦EMERGENCE?⟧ Emergence remains asserted before it is observed

This criticism does not stand against the inspected dnav3 claims because dnav3
does not rely on emergence as its principal explanation.

The cognitive package is explicit about what is authored:

- the model proposes a framing, acts, requirements, and expectations;
- the runtime validates and places them;
- the world reports outcomes;
- exact comparison settles claims;
- counted edges update;
- activation and retrieval select precedent;
- a reconciler replans when the world exposes missing prerequisites.

The behavior is described as a designed loop with measurable consequences, not
as intelligence mysteriously emerging from recursive physics. The source calls
the Being a “walking skeleton” and the package an active cognition experiment.

There may eventually be emergent effects inside the interaction of memory,
activation, prediction, and world feedback. No such effect needs to be accepted
for dnav3's current thesis to be evaluated. Applying the old criticism would
attack a claim this version is not presently using as its foundation.

Status: **not applicable to the central dnav3 claim**.

## ⟦PRODUCT≠SCIENCE⟧ The boundary between product language and scientific language remains unclear

This criticism no longer stands as a general characterization of the supplied
dnav3 materials.

The boundaries are unusually explicit:

- dnav3 calls itself an active cognition experiment.
- The interpretation learning document is labeled `Status: proposal`.
- Its uncommitted formulas and deferred mechanisms are identified as such.
- The distributed-agent package says its own name is forward-looking and admits
  that both worlds are local and in-process.
- The traversal package identifies itself as a prototype and distinguishes
  itself from the live relation kernel.
- The newer runner says it is currently unreachable and warns readers not to
  treat its tests as live-runtime evidence.
- The user-agent package describes its current implementation as a minimal
  boundary log rather than artificial human cognition.

This is precisely the labeling discipline the original criticism requested:
proposal, experiment, prototype, current implementation, forward-looking name,
and deferred work are not silently presented as the same evidentiary category.

Individual nouns still outrun their mechanisms, and the system remains far from
production. Those concerns have their own symbols. But the particular criticism
that Skyra is failing to distinguish product ambition, implemented mechanism,
and research hypothesis is no longer fair as a blanket statement about dnav3.

Status: **answered in the inspected documentation, subject to continued
discipline**.

## Summary

The symbols that no longer stand in their original form are:

```text
⟦FEEDBACK↺⟧
⟦LAW≈CONVENTION⟧
⟦CLAIM¬FALSIFIED⟧
⟦EMERGENCE?⟧
⟦PRODUCT≠SCIENCE⟧
```

Retiring these criticisms is not generosity. It is required accuracy. dnav3
changed the evidence beneath them, and the critic must change with it.
