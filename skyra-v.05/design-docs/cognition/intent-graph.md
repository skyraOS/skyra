# Intent Graph

## What It Is

The intent graph is the being's prediction of what it needs to do next. Expressed as XML, emitted on every thought (every provider call). The being can change it whenever it wants. It's the being orienting its own substrate — steering its traversal from inside.

## Structure

Actions without `<then>` fire simultaneously:

```
<engage>school</engage>
<look>notebook</look>
```

Both fire at the same time. The being wants all of it at once.

`<then>` creates a sequence — the being gets a chance to take in information before firing the next action:

```
<engage>school</engage>
<look>notebook</look>
<then>
<engage>library</engage>
```

The being engages school and looks at the notebook simultaneously, processes what comes back, then engages the library. `<then>` is a breath — the difference between reflex and deliberation.

## Dynamics

The intent graph gets sent on every thought. It evolves mid-traversal. The being engages something, sees something unexpected, rewrites the intent graph. The prediction updates because the observation changed. The being is continuously re-orienting based on what it's encountering.

The declared targets in the intent graph get activation boosts in the topology. The being's prediction of where it needs to go shapes what actually activates. The being is not passively following weights — it's actively modulating the activation landscape through intent.

## What This Is

The being is predicting its own future. The predictions shape the topology. That's agency.

This connects to the system prompt: "What you observe drives what you think. What you think drives what you can use." The intent graph is the think step made explicit — the being's thought includes its prediction, and that prediction is what activates the next capability.

## Predictions as Feedback Loops

The prediction is on the Relation. The outcome is what actually happened. The delta between them is the learning signal.

The being predicted a sequence. Reality returned something different. The being re-oriented. That re-orientation — the gap between what it predicted and what it had to change to — is data. Cells the original prediction activated but the revised prediction abandoned get the signal: wrong direction. Cells the revised prediction found get: right direction. Weight updates on the return path encode the difference.

Over time the being's predictions get better because the topology remembers which predictions led to re-orientation (costly, signal wasted on wrong paths) and which ones held (efficient, straight to the target). The being develops intuition — predictions that fire from warm paths instead of cold guesses.

No separate feedback mechanism. The prediction is the signal. The outcome is the weight update. The delta is the learning. Same `Realize()`.

## Skills Are Reflexes

Predictions that keep holding under pressure stop being predictions. They become the warm path the being reaches for without deliberating. That's a skill.

Not a file. Not a label. Not an md file an agent reads. A skill is a path through the topology that got reinforced because the prediction kept being right. The transition from deliberate to automatic. From reasoning to reflex.

When activation on a path is high enough, Express competence reads as motor — straight to act. No compress pass. No provider call. The being doesn't need to think about it because the topology already knows the answer. The prediction is baked into the weights.

A cold path needs a think pass — the being reasons through it, compress fires, the provider collapses the context. A warm path needs less. A hot path — a skill — fires directly. Observe picks up the signal, activation skips compress, motor executes. Reflex.

Competence already handles this. The cell reads itself. If activation is high enough, Express reads as motor instead of compress. Skills aren't a feature to build. They're what happens when a path gets hot enough to skip thinking.

And when a skill's prediction fails for the first time — the path that always worked doesn't work — the re-orientation creates a branch. New path, new prediction, new feedback. The skill grows a new edge. It doesn't break. It adapts.
