# Attention

## Why a Being is Receptive

From first principles — reasons a being notices incoming signal:

- **Idle** — not doing anything. No active traversals. Full attention available. At rest = maximally receptive.
- **Relevance** — the signal is about something the being is currently thinking about. Already active in the topology.
- **Relationship** — it's from someone the being has strong weight with. Their signal always penetrates.
- **Recency** — the being was just talking to this source. The exchange is warm.
- **Need** — the being is stuck. Its intent graph has an unresolved step. A signal that fills the gap gets through.
- **Novelty** — the signal is about something the being has no weight on. Surprising. Attention spikes.
- **Urgency** — the signal carries alarm. Penetrates regardless of state.

## The Equation

```
reception = sender_activation * receiver_attention
```

Sender activation scores how strongly the impulse points at the target. Receiver attention scores how receptive the target is right now. Both must be high for the signal to land.
