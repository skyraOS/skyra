# Skyra — The Cognitive Cycle (as specced, 2026-06-20)

A standalone, cold-readable summary of the cognitive architecture as designed. Supersedes
`2026-06-16.md` where they differ (the big changes since: Coherence → Global; two binding
realities → self/other agents; parents/future edge dropped; edge-owned activation; the
groupings/gists pipeline; concurrent self/other at closure + the cascade orchestration).

---

## The players

**Membrane** — the being's sensorimotor boundary. `Observe` = attention gate (pass-through
stub for MVP), `Express` = motor. `Content` = the root drive. `Children` = Global.

**Global** — the orchestrator. Three jobs: **vote tally**, **tie-break**, **grouping**. Two
agents (`self`, `other`), each its own descent/ascent. Its `Children()` are **groupings**, not
raw setpoints. Visibility is scoped: it sees only crystallized understandings of completed
setpoints + potential gists of active groupings — never raw, ungrouped, unresolved setpoints
(that altitude is the kernel + the raw predict/score layer below it).

**Setpoints** — *predictions that came true*. **One node:** `Content()` = the **understanding**
(the crystallized gist — what it means); `Children()` = the **entities** involved + responses +
actions + stresses (the surroundings snapshot + evidence at closure). Past-facing only; no
parent/future edge. Created when the predicted **self AND other both close**. A setpoint's
**state** is a tower of crystallized layers (levels n, n-1, …).

**Entities** — person/place/thing, each a cell with a stable **ID**. `Children()` = the setpoints
stamped on them **at completion** (the write-time invariant). They're the reverse-index hub: the
"reverse read" is **a wave fired from the surroundings, not a hash** — it spreads
`understanding → entity → understanding …`, bounded by amplitude (associative recall = spreading
activation, free from the kernel). Decay automatic (edge activation + threshold); salience falls
out of branching (hub entities spread thin). Entity *resolution* (perceived → stable id) is the
real work. See `design-docs/cognition/entities-and-surroundings.md`.

**Surroundings map** — on the slice; the current active entities, updated on every `perceive`. The
query for the reverse read and the snapshot a setpoint stamps at completion.

**Groupings** — Global's index over active setpoints: higher-order setpoints (longer horizon =
more abstract). They *are* the future, applied top-down as context. Recursive (group the
groupings), loosely coupled, no named tiers. Each carries a **gist**. No explicit alignment —
relevance is read off the attention Global keeps paying it.

**Gist / Understanding / Stress** — a gist is *an understanding that hasn't crystallized yet*.
On success it becomes a durable **Understanding**; on failure cascade, a **Stress**. Same
deposit-then-crystallize move, opposite sign.

**Working-memory buffer** — transient activation on the slice; exists only while a prediction
is active. Holds incoming evidence + recent *failed* predictions (the expected-vs-actual gap).
On closure its contents become children of the resulting Understanding (success) or Stress
(failure).

**Action store** — the justification index (`assumption-id → actions justified by it`); the
self-pass's substrate.

## The substrate

**Kernel** — pure transport. Iterative in-plane DFS (actor crossing deleted), amplitude
attenuation per depth (×0.9), amplitude divided across branches, visited-once rail, threshold
gate, perception-invariant return (every wave's result goes back to its origin). The kernel
never reads content or scoring — it keys an activation record off the map key it's handed and
asks the cell to `Activate()`.

**Activation — edge-owned (synaptic model).** A record belongs to a *connection*, not a node:
A→B and C→B are independent weights. Records live on the slice, keyed by the **map key** (edge
identity), not the child's ID. The cell owns *which* edges exist (adjacency); the slice owns
*how strong* each is.

```
bump = IndexScore × Alignment × stress_modulator
```
- fed into two EMAs: **Identity** (α=0.02, the groove) and **Familiarity** (α=0.1, recent)
- **signed** (negative alignment → negative bump); **decay → 0 from both directions**
- `Alignment` is **lagged** — written from the previous closure scoring
- `IndexScore` = product of matching typed inverted-index dimensions (person/place/thing)
- stress modulates bump magnitude and stickiness (high-stress negatives groove harder / decay slower)
- cell-owned: the kernel calls `Activate()`, gets a number, never sees the equation

**Edges vs. potentials.** Direct neighbors are declared edges (in `Children()`, edge-keyed).
Transitive reach is a **potential** — a path composition (`ab ⊗ bc`) computed at traversal time
via attenuation, A-specific because the amplitude started at A, **not stored**. A potential
that grades above the keep-threshold **crystallizes** into a declared edge (Hebbian — the cell
adds the target to its own `Children()`). Scored potentials that don't crystallize — including
negatives — persist as **history**: edge-keyed records with no adjacency; they hold their value
but the kernel never walks them.

## The cycle

### Step 0 — Observe / grouping (Global, 1 LLM call)
On impulse: score it against the **active groupings** in the working-memory buffer's context
(buffer-vs-grouping). **Skips entirely if there's no completed setpoint** (cold start has
nothing to abstract over). The chosen grouping becomes the **top-down context** for the rest of
the cycle — each active setpoint is read under whatever long-term goal is active.

### Step 1 — Filter (kernel, no LLM, pure math)
The kernel walks the setpoint topology, scoring `activation × stress × alignment` (cell-owned).
Impulse + warm activation cut the field to survivors (> threshold). Survivors assemble the
working-memory buffer on the slice.

### Step 2 — Per-grouping self/other (concurrent at closure)
**One predict-score-close pass per active grouping** (not per setpoint), with the grouping's
constituent setpoints as *material*. Grouping width sets explore/exploit (narrow = more
spillover to explore; wide = thinner attention, exploit).

**Frame for the pass:** high-activation history of the grouping's setpoints + currently
selected setpoints + Global's **gist** (the framing) + predictions carried from the previous
pass on the slice.

Self and other fire **concurrently** — independent retrospective questions:
- **self:** did my previous action close the prediction I committed to?
- **other:** did this impulse close the other's open setpoint?

N ≥ 2 waves, each a separate-slice agent reading a **snapshot** of the shared frame; Global owns
the buffer and integrates only at the join. Safe by construction: **snapshot-in, join-out**.
(Concurrent at *closure*; still ordered other→self at *prediction* — "self relies on other.")

- **predict / grade** — produce the grouping's prediction (lives in the WM buffer); grade
  against incoming evidence (broadcast WM, each constituent self-selects relevance, alignment
  accumulates).
- **closure** — on proven-true: the prediction graduates into a **new setpoint at level n**;
  constituents become its **children (n-1)**; the gist crystallizes into a durable
  **Understanding**; setpoint(n) becomes eligible as a constituent at n+1 (**stacking** — the
  abstraction pyramid forms by resolved understandings becoming raw material, not a merge pass).

### Step 3 — Orchestration: route the 2-D outcome (Global, code)
The concurrent waves return independently → outcome is 2-D (self × other). A **pure-code
selector** routes on `(stress-delta, closure-grade)` — **cascade dominates**:

1. **Any cascade → reconcile (failure).** Unstable ground invalidates any closure. The cascade
   is a **failure wave**: it propagates *down* the setpoint's tower, attenuating, until it hits
   **solid ground** (~0.2 threshold). The **delta** (span from contradiction to solid ground)
   is **rolled back out of the setpoint's state** (TMS backtracking) and **deposited into WM**.
   The popped span becomes potential groupings again → Global reorganizes → gist crystallizes as
   a **Stress**. Nothing deleted — rollback, recoverable.
2. **Else any closure → record + continue (success).** Recurse to Global, regroup, kick the next
   wave (predict → self); gist → Understanding.
3. **Else open → act + await.** Re-predict → feed self → self emits a setpoint action → emit and
   await the next impulse (predict-act-perceive). Bounded by amplitude; deliberation is a
   bounded knob.

Routing never calls an LLM; inference lives only in each branch's work.

### Step 4 — Vote (Global, no LLM, pure math)
Hopfield weighted sum over the scores. Threshold crossing → action. No clear crossing → Global
fires (tie-break / gather). Scores feed back into the activation records for the next filter
pass (alignment is lagged).

## The prediction-time recovery loop (when a prediction fails)

Distinct from closure concurrency — this is the **ordered** other→self path.

Ascent grading fires one inference with `setpoint + assumptions + predictions + response`. A
**relevance gate** first (irrelevant → unchanged, the common case), else the **prediction ×
assumption 2×2**:

| | assumption right | assumption wrong |
|---|---|---|
| **prediction right** | 3 — match (reinforce) | 4 — got lucky (don't reinforce; keep prediction) |
| **prediction wrong** | 1 — missing (gather "what am I missing?") | 2 — false ground (revise assumption, recurse) |

**Two-stage repair:** Stage 1 (other) revises beliefs down to solid ground; handoff; Stage 2
(self) replans actions against the corrected target. You never fix a prediction — you fix the
assumption that produced it.

**Two learning channels, kept separate:** alignment miss (object-level, revise, per-cycle,
transient) vs. system failure (meta-level, "I broke my own operating rules," persistent prompt
channel, top ~5 magnitude-weighted).

## The threshold family

| Threshold | Job | Value |
|---|---|---|
| traversal survival | kernel gate for descent | `sl.Threshold()` |
| keep / crystallization | add to children; path→edge | ~0.1 |
| solid ground / cascade-stop | where a cascade halts | ~0.2 (TBD) |

Invariant: **solid-ground > crystallization** (a cascade halts on something genuinely held).
Exact values empirical.

## The economy (emergent, not designed)

- **crystallize-on-success** — potential → stable (paths → edges, gists → Understandings)
- **decay-without-reinforcement** — unused groupings/edges fade
- **cascade-on-failure** — stable → potential (de-crystallization, the inverse)

The graph **forms, prunes, and reorganizes**. A grouping is implicitly a bet that compressing
these setpoints pays off in a reusable concept; resolve → permanent stackable Understanding,
never resolve → decay. Nobody wrote a reward function for "build abstractions" — it falls out of
these three interacting.
