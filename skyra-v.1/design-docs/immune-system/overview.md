# Immune System — Structural Quality Control

## Status

**Planning — a new, separate mechanism**, distinct from the cognition layer. Where cognition *learns* (alignment) and *corrects* (the fast nervous-system error feedback), the immune system **prunes** — it eliminates cells that persistently fail. Slow, structural, eliminative. Consumes the **system-failure signal** from [error-recovery-loop.md](../cognition/error-recovery-loop.md#two-learning-channels--alignment-vs-system-failure).

## What it is

The body handles "bad firing" with two systems on two timescales:

- **Nervous (fast, corrective):** detect the error, encode it hard, feed it back as a persistent lesson. In Skyra: **Coherence** (ACC error-monitoring — the error-related-negativity analog) + **Emotion** (egregious errors persist). Already in the cognition layer (the system-failure channel).
- **Immune (slow, structural):** eliminate the component that keeps misfiring. Biology: **apoptosis** (programmed cell death), **negative selection** (delete dangerous components before they cause harm), **microglial synaptic pruning** (the brain's own immune cells eat bad/weak synapses). **This is that system** — it deletes persistently-failing cells.

## The key distinction: low-relevance ≠ bad

The project's standing principle is "suppress, don't delete" — low-activation cells fade but stay (resurfaceable). The immune system is the **legitimate exception**, and the line is sharp:

- **Low-relevance → suppress.** Not bad, just not active now. Fades, can resurface. (Curation / the comparator.)
- **Persistently-failing → prune.** A cell that *repeatedly causes system failures* — not alignment misses, **system failures** (vacuity, hallucination, gaming, serving the letter not the spirit) — gets **deleted.** Like a misbehaving cell apoptosed, a self-attacking T-cell deleted, a bad synapse pruned.

Two different fates for two different reasons: **suppression is about *attention*; pruning is about *health*.**

## What it consumes

The **system-failure signal** ([error-recovery-loop.md → Two Learning Channels](../cognition/error-recovery-loop.md#two-learning-channels--alignment-vs-system-failure)). Same signal, two consumers:
- **Nervous channel** → turns repeated system failures into a *persistent lesson* (correct the operation).
- **Immune channel** → when those failures are attributed to a specific cell that keeps producing them, into a *prune* (eliminate the source).

## Where it sits — the topology's lifecycle

Skyra already has the build-up direction: the **promotion gradient** (trace → understanding → skill; cells reinforce and climb). The immune prune is the **tear-down** direction. Together they're the full morphogenetic lifecycle — cells are born, promoted, and apoptosed. Levin's morphogenesis sculpts form by *both* growth and programmed death; pruning isn't a bug-fix bolt-on, it's the other half of how the topology keeps the right shape.

## Open questions

- **"Persistently failing" — the threshold.** How many system failures attributed to a cell, over what window, before it's pruned? Immune tolerance: don't apoptose on one bad event (that's the amygdala/Emotion's job — encode it). Prune on a *pattern*.
- **What gets pruned — the cell or its edges?** Whole-cell apoptosis vs. synaptic pruning (remove the bad connections, keep the cell). Biology does both.
- **Reversibility.** Apoptosis is irreversible. Is a prune? Or is it a *hard* suppression with a very long decay (recoverable) vs. a *true* delete (gone)? The "nothing deleted" principle argues for the former; biology's apoptosis argues for the latter. Likely fork: prune *edges* reversibly, delete *cells* only after a long tolerance window.
- **Autoimmunity — the failure mode of the failure-handler.** An immune system that mis-prunes attacks the self (deletes *good* cells). What is the regulatory check (the Treg analog) that keeps the prune from eating healthy topology? This is the dangerous edge: a quality-control system that itself misfires is worse than none.
