# Skyra — Inner DNA Design Summary

**Date:** 2026-06-16 · **Purpose:** a standalone, reviewable summary of the cognitive-architecture design landed this session. Written to be read cold (no specs required), e.g. for an outside review pass. Status: **design, not built** (see end).

---

## What Skyra is

Skyra is a personal-AI runtime where **cognition is topology + traversal, not a prompt chain.** A single interface (`Space`) describes everything — the world, a being, and every cell inside it — and a single kernel (the "Relation") walks that topology as a wave. The being's "mind" is an explicit, inspectable graph of **setpoint cells**; an LLM is the *operator* over that graph (it scores and generates against an assembled "present"), **not** the seat of the model. The core bet: externalize the latent space that JEPA-style world-models keep *inside* a network, make it a symbolic topology, and run an LLM as the per-step operator over it. The result sits deliberately between JEPA (predict in abstract space, hold predictions uncollapsed, energy = alignment) and LLMs (language, knowledge, interpretability) — and it maps onto LeCun's autonomous-machine-intelligence modules (perception / world-model / cost / actor), with the **root drive = the intrinsic cost**. Because the operations are abstract, the LLM is swappable for a JEPA-latent core later; only the operator changes, not the architecture.

## Cell anatomy

Everything is a `Space`. The being's brain nests:

```
Membrane (the boundary; Content = the root drive)
 └── Coherence (the central cell / global workspace)
      ├── Temporal binding reality   { self, other }
      └── Episodic binding reality    { self, other }
            └── each holds Setpoints (the recursive primitive)
 Action store (past actions indexed by the assumptions that justified them)
```

The through-line of the session: **the right moves removed cells.** Motivation, Goal, Attention, Prediction, Prospective, and WorkingMemory all dissolved into *methods*, *faces*, or *held state* of the cells above — they are not their own cells.

## The setpoint (the recursive primitive)

A setpoint is the workhorse node. It holds:
- **assumptions** — durable, *fixable* ground (what it takes as true)
- **predictions** — *derived* output of the assumptions (expected self-model + other-model)

Its `Children()` are its **dependency/assumption setpoints**, so the graph recurses; it bottoms out at a **sensory leaf** (a setpoint whose alignment is read straight from the world). Two faces:
- **Observe (descent) = Motivation = gate** — check the dependencies/assumptions underneath.
- **Express (ascent) = Goal = build** — compose alignment toward closure.

**Alignment** is a signed −1..1 scalar (the LLM's judgment of how close the world is to the setpoint's observable `satisfied-when`). On the *self* side, **alignment IS the confidence** (the self-prediction is goal-relative by construction). Closure is **observed, not asserted** — confirmable only by reading the world/other back, never declared by the being (this is what stops the "buy the plane ticket and call the vacation done" failure).

## Assumptions = a truth-maintenance system

Dependencies are best understood as **assumptions**: setpoints held *true by default, defeasibly, until contradicted*. Three sources: **persistence** (previously verified, assumed to persist — the frame problem), **default** (high-prior, never verified — default logic), **promoted prediction** (a prediction reliable enough to stop checking). Status is a stamp `{assumed, verified, predicted, refuted}`. Defeating an assumption **cascades** to everything that depended on it (a classic TMS). **Coherence** is the monitor that catches the contradiction.

## Self / other (Theory of Mind)

Every prediction has two faces: the AI's **own** setpoint (self) and its **model of the other** (the interlocutor). Keeping them separate is Theory of Mind; collapsing them is the canonical LLM failure (projecting your own knowledge onto the user).

- **Perspective is structural, not a tag.** Each binding reality has **separate self/other sub-realities** — four hard-separate stores. (A write-time "stamp" was considered and rejected for this boundary: a stamp is a *soft* separation that can bleed; the ToM boundary is too load-bearing to leave to a discipline that can be mis-applied. Structure makes the bleed impossible.)
- **As methods:** descent (Observe) = model the **other**; ascent (Express) = produce the **self**.
- **Self relies on other** (intersubjectivity — Mead/Vygotsky/Hegel): you can't know what *your* setpoint must be until you've inferred *theirs*. Other-first. The top of the other-model is the AI's best inference of the human's true goal — i.e. the alignment target is *inferred across a perspective gap*, not handed over directly.

## The two binding realities

Same memory primitive, two access patterns (Tulving's semantic/episodic; narrow/wide attention; local/global processing — three registers, one cut):

- **Temporal** (focused / deep / **one active at a time**) — the **spotlight**. Goes deep within a single setpoint; "one active" is the attention bottleneck *and* the coherence requirement (a being needs one throughline). Holds discrete focus-switches.
- **Episodic** (distributed / weighted / wide) — the **field**. Energy spread across setpoints by activation; the diffuse background.

**Coupling:** Episodic = the field; Temporal = the spotlight on the field's peak. **What moves the spotlight:**
- **Bottom-up salience** = `person/place/thing` (entity **reverse-lookup** / location-based memory) × `stress` (state-dependent recall: stressful setpoints raise other stress-congruent setpoints + their history). Combined as a **product across active dimensions** (any dimension can fire alone).
- **Top-down bias** = Coherence bumps/decays each setpoint vs. working memory.
- **The spotlight moves** when the current setpoint's substructure **can no longer compose** under it (= the error-recovery repair loop gives up) — with **hysteresis** so a momentary dip doesn't thrash it.

## Coherence (the central cell / global workspace)

Coherence orchestrates the two realities — it *is* a Global Workspace (the ACC/frontopolar labels dissolve into this function):
- **Observe = synthesize + broadcast + hold.** Reads both realities' assumptions+predictions (self+other), produces a synthesis ("what went right / what went wrong"), broadcasts it back to each reality, and **holds it**.
- **Express = the verdict / vote.** Casts a deciding vote **only when the realities disagree** (tie-break); stays silent on consensus.

The synthesis Coherence holds **is working memory** — specifically Baddeley's **episodic buffer** (the transient, integrative "what's happening now"). It is a **sliding window** over recent activity (overlapping — one event sits in several consecutive windows = multi-context binding, a feature, not duplicated storage). **The windowing is Coherence's**, not the binding realities' (they are durable stores). This dissolved the separate WorkingMemory cell.

## The membrane (sensorimotor boundary)

Not pure routing — it owns the one attention field, read twice:
- **Observe = the attention gate.** "Does this catch my attention?" — a relevance check (cheap resonance first, alignment if borderline) against working memory. Caught → pass up; not → discard.
- **Express = the motor.** Fires the action when the returned votes are **above threshold and agree** — basal-ganglia coincidence detection. It **never breaks ties** (that is Coherence).

## The cognitive cycle (both realities run it each turn)

**Descent — fix prediction-assumptions.** Compare over three text blocks: `assumptions | predictions | results`. Where prediction ≠ result, fix the *assumption* that produced the bad prediction (predictions are derived; you never fix a prediction). Recurse down the assumption lineage to solid ground; **depth is surprise-proportional** (cheap call only at low-alignment nodes, floored by the sensory leaf and amplitude attenuation).

**Ascent — grade.** One inference fires with `setpoint + assumptions + predictions + response`. First a **relevance gate**: if the response doesn't bear on this setpoint (alignment ≈ 0) → **unchanged** (carry over as-is — the common case, and a correctness guard). If relevant, it grades two axes *independently against the response* → the **prediction × assumption 2×2**:

| | assumption right | assumption wrong |
|---|---|---|
| **prediction right** | **match** → reinforce | **got lucky** (Gettier — true-but-wrong-reason → *don't* reinforce the assumption; keep the prediction) |
| **prediction wrong** | **missing** → gather ("what am I missing?", latent-variable generation) | **false ground** → revise the assumption, recurse |

Grading both axes independently is *why* "got lucky" is detectable at all — and why the system does not overfit to spurious correlations.

**Two-stage repair (belief-then-plan):** Stage 1 (the **other**-pass) revises beliefs and outputs a corrected prediction + the bad-assumption ids; the handoff passes those to Stage 2 (the **self**-pass), which gathers the past actions that depended on the bad assumptions (via the **Action** store's justification index), grades them, and produces corrected candidate actions.

**Generation and grading are one inference call** (a shared trunk — a second call would be a disconnected critic that drifts). **Predictions stay latent** (held with confidence) and **only collapse at the vote.**

## Gating action — voting + convergence

The realities **vote** with their self-models (alignment-as-confidence). Collapse happens at the vote:
- **Consensus** → the **membrane** fires the action (basal-ganglia coincidence, fast path, no center needed).
- **Conflict** → **Coherence** engages (ACC, slow path) — casts the deciding vote or gathers ("what would resolve this?") before committing.

This maps onto the cortico-basal-ganglia loop: cortical regions (the realities) bid; the basal ganglia (membrane Express) tallies and fires on agreement; the ACC (Coherence) engages only on conflict.

## State & the present

- Each reality keeps `category-prediction-active` on the slice — refs to **carried-over** assumptions/predictions (still active) + **new** cells. This is the per-turn active set the cycle reads from and writes back to.
- Coherence keeps the **sliding window** (working memory).
- The "present" handed to the LLM is a **read-time transform** of the slice — *decoupled from storage order*, and where temporality is made **explicit** (LLMs treat sequence-position as a learned recency convention, not real time; an assembled topology-present is not chronological, so time must be stamped, not inferred from position).

## Status & open questions

This is **design, not built.** The kernel runs (traversal, activation, the perception-invariant); the cognitive layer is specced and validated only as "an LLM scoring hand-built presents." The **MVP** to reach first contact is small: membrane + one setpoint + Coherence + the atomic generate+align call + boot — enough to run the comparator on a *traversed* frame instead of a hand-built one.

Key open items:
- **State-4 ("got lucky") handling** — how to avoid reinforcing the lucky assumption without discarding the correct prediction.
- **Spotlight move/hold hysteresis** — when the focus switches vs. perseveres.
- **Naming** of the focused reality — "temporal" vs. "categorical/semantic."
- **Environment layer** for outward firing — membrane vs. world.
- **Setpoint: one cell or two** — whether Motivation/Goal are two faces of one cell or two cells.
- **Salience combination** beyond product (does stress override an irrelevant entity match?).
