# Remaining boundaries

DNA-18.5 exercises recoverable loop rejection, a correlated context activation
through B's own horizon/sink, and capability-mediated creation of a new usable
address. RootSink is now a frame destination separate from Egress. The simplified
FrameID and executable contracts are explicit decisions in DECISIONS.md.

Sockets, save/resume, general activation envelopes, arbitrary multi-child joins,
provider/OS containment, fairness/backpressure, crash recovery, signing and reliable
delivery remain deferred. No context is architecturally prohibited from restoration
or exposure through an executable; restoration would need fresh authority and
reconciliation with new bindings/reservations.

The activation peer serializes calls and uses one fixed echo entry profile. Its
caller/B correlation is transient peer state, with no persistence or recovery.
The creation peer and its callback are trusted, scoped host mediation. This proves
one new echo address, not arbitrary executable synthesis, delegation policies,
reparenting or general provisioning. The dedicated virtual/actual creation slots
are fixture choices. A retired generated executable remains in Memory, while its
live reservation and binding are revoked. Allocation exhaustion is not modeled.

Missing contexts, unexpected endpoint loss and committed-send errors invalidate
this bounded runtime and cancel the loop. Move rejection is isolated; independent
recovery from a crashed context/provider is not demonstrated. Intentional rebind
abandons old pending frames and closes its old ingress; callers must attach any
replacement input. Reconciliation/faulting of abandoned frames remains open.

Transport sends can block workers. Two workers demonstrate bounded independent
progress, not unlimited progress when both block. Retained frames/request IDs are
unbounded, and committed sends are neither retried nor transactionally durable.
Engram compilation, OS/preparation services and the production fence interfaces
remain absent; the divergence inventory in DECISIONS.md distinguishes deferred
layers from changed contracts. Fault strings do not settle the open taxonomy.
