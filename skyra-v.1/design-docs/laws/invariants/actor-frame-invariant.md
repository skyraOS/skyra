# The Actor Frame Invariant

## Status

**Foundational.** The companion to the [Perception Invariant](perception-invariant.md): that one governs how a wave's result returns as perception; this one governs where each wave begins. Together they bound outward action and inward return. Must hold unchanged through v.1.

## The Invariant

> **Thought roots in the actor. Action roots in the actor's location. Return perception roots in the actor.**

(*Actor*, *being*, *observer*, and *wave owner* are one thing seen from different sides — the actor when it acts, the observer when it perceives, the being ontologically. This document says "actor.")

## What It Means

`Realize(origin, traversed, impulse)` carries two independent axes. `origin` is the **actor** — the wave's owner and the address its result returns to. `traversed` is the **root realm** the wave walks. The actor is always `origin`. Only `traversed` varies:

- **Thought** — `Realize(self, self, impulse)`. Owner and root coincide; the actor walks its own interior. No environment is invoked.
- **Action** — `world.observe(self, content)` → `Realize(self, pointOf(self), content)`. Owner stays the actor; the root becomes the actor's location. The wave propagates from where the actor stands; the environment translates owner → location through the occupancy it is authoritative for.
- **Return perception** — `Realize(origin, origin, resultImpulse)`. The result returns by rooting a new inward wave in the actor, wherever the outward wave went. This line is the [Perception Invariant](perception-invariant.md).

## The Actor Is the Fixed Point

The actor does not move within a wave. It owns the wave (`origin`) and it is the root of the return perception wave — both fixed. The *only* thing that moves is the outward root: inward to its interior for thought, or outward to its location for action.

This is the clean form of two facts we found separately:

- **Reach goes outward; perception comes home.** Action propagates from the actor's location into the world; the result starts a return wave through the actor. Return is by owner, propagation is by location — they are different axes, so an actor processes the result of its act no matter how far the wave (or the actor) has moved since.
- **The actor is the frame.** Because every wave is owned by, and returns to, the actor, every quantity a wave measures is measured *in the actor's frame*. That is why thought, action, distance, and time are all relative to the actor — see the [Local Relativity Invariant](local-relativity-invariant.md). This invariant is what makes the actor the frame local relativity is stated against.

## Why the Root Must Vary

Thought and action cannot share a root once space conducts. Thought is the actor reasoning over its own interior, so it roots on the actor. Action must *propagate across the world* to have an effect, so it must root at a world point — the actor's location — and travel outward from there. An act rooted on the actor's interior would never leave the actor. The split is not a convenience; it is what lets an actor act on a world it is only a small part of.

A consequence: **an actor with no location cannot act outward.** There is nowhere for the wave to root. To act in a world, an actor must be placed in it; `pointOf(self)` must resolve.

## Holds Recursively

The invariant names only *actor*, *location*, and *return perception* — all of which exist at every level of the nesting. A specialist inside a being thinks by rooting on itself, acts by rooting at its location in the being's inner world, and processes the result through a return wave rooted in itself — exactly as a top-level being does against the outer world. Stated only in terms present at every level, it is automatically recursive. The rule is written once.
