## STANDS WITH EDITS — the core holds, named claims died

### `continuation-language/continuation-language.md`

The single most valuable document to carry, and it needs three specific corrections.

```text
HOLDS      the frame is supplied, not invented · nothing may appear from nowhere · types are
           contracts grounded by provenance, never self-declared · contract mismatch is
           structural failure requiring no universal Error · three distinct failure boundaries ·
           the parent owns the parser · the language remains intentionally boring · the
           16-point acceptance test, minus items 6 and 7.

RETIRED    "every child call is Decide → Reach → Possibility → Remember." A child call is ONE
           MOVE with a request header. Reach is a stage; Decide is a header value.

RETIRED    "TYPED ASSIGNMENT IS LOCAL REMEMBER" and "RETURN IS OUTWARD REMEMBER."
           REMEMBER IS NOW ROOT-ONLY AND BULK. These two operations are real and still need
           names — a local binding is not a durable act, and the document's own instinct that
           they are the same verb at two boundaries is what the new model rejects.

ANSWERED   "whether independent calls run concurrently must be explicit… source layout alone must
           not accidentally make scheduler timing semantic." SETTLED: sequence is a DEPENDENCY,
           not a schedule. Writing `A; B` does not order them.
```

## The pattern worth noticing

```text
DIED       every document whose subject was A SURFACE — which methods exist, what they return,
           what types carry them.
LIVED      every document whose subject was A CONSTRAINT — what may not be forged, what may not
           appear from nowhere, what the substrate can prove, what the kernel may not read.
```

That is not luck. A surface is a bet on a machine; a constraint is a claim about causality, and
the pivot changed the machine without changing the causality. It is also a reason to expect the
next pivot to cost the same things again — the `move.md` and `primitives.md` decompositions are
surface documents, and should be held as loosely as this set's were.
